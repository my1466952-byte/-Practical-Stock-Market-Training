## 📚 Explore Different Areas of Market Education

The stock market includes several areas of learning, and each requires a different set of skills. Understanding these areas helps learners identify which subjects match their interests and financial goals.

### 1. Technical Analysis

Technical analysis focuses on:

- Price charts and candlestick patterns
- Trendlines and market trends
- Technical indicators
- Support and resistance levels
- Trading volume and price behaviour

These tools help traders study market behaviour and evaluate potential opportunities.

### 2. Fundamental Analysis

Fundamental analysis involves examining:

- Company earnings and financial statements
- Valuation ratios
- Business performance
- Company growth prospects
- Financial health and industry conditions

It helps investors evaluate businesses before making investment decisions.

### 3. Options Trading

Learners interested in derivatives can explore:

- Option contracts
- Call and put options
- Strike prices and expiry dates
- Options trading strategies
- Risk management techniques

Building knowledge gradually is important before applying advanced techniques in live markets.

## 📊 Develop Discipline Alongside Market Knowledge

Knowing trading strategies is only one part of market education. Risk management, patience, and emotional discipline are equally important.

Important concepts include:

- Position sizing
- Stop-loss orders
- Risk-reward ratios
- Trading psychology
- Maintaining a trading journal
- Reviewing previous trading decisions

Practising chart analysis and reviewing past decisions can improve analytical thinking. No strategy can guarantee profits, and market conditions can change unexpectedly.
